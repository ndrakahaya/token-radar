HOW TO READ THE RADAR

A model, not a forecast.

Implied value is a comparative estimate of fully diluted token value. It is not a guarantee, a price target, or financial advice.

Valuation framework

Implied value = protocol TVL × calibrated value-to-TVL multiple

The multiple reflects a protocol’s category economics, observed peer valuations, current growth, and maturity. Growth and scale can support a higher multiple; execution risk, concentration, and uncertain token utility constrain it. The result is a scenario estimate, not a promise of an airdrop or token launch.

Calibration & caveats

Comparable protocols are grouped by business model and adjusted for differences in adoption and lifecycle. TVL and growth are snapshots and may move quickly; TVL can include incentives, recursive positions, or assets that are not economically independent. Model outputs are rounded and should be compared as ranges of relative potential, not precise outcomes.
